# AIONOS Promotion and Career Progression Policy

Effective date: 1 April 2026  
Owner: People Operations with department heads.

## Purpose

Promotions recognise sustained higher-level work, not time served. This policy covers movements across Production, IT/IS, Sales, Software Engineering, Admin Offices, and the Executive Office, including product career paths aligned to UniStack, UniWeave, UniScale, and UniProtect.

## Promotion windows

- Primary window: March–April, aligned with compensation review.
- Secondary window: September, for business-critical backfills and exceptional Exceeds talent.
- Out-of-cycle promotions require the department head and Head of People.

## Minimum eligibility

An employee is eligible for consideration only if all of the following are true:

1. Active employment status (not terminated, not in notice unless a pre-approved internal move).
2. At least 12 months in the current Position, or 9 months with an Exceeds rating and department-head waiver.
3. Latest PerformanceScore is Fully Meets or Exceeds. Needs Improvement and PIP are not eligible.
4. Engagement survey at or above 3.5 on the most recent record.
5. Unplanned Absences at or below 12 in the trailing 12 months, unless a documented medical exception exists.
6. No open formal disciplinary case.

## Evidence pack

The manager submits:

- Updated job description for the target Position.
- Two examples of work already performed at the higher level (for engineers: production systems; for UniProtect: incident leadership; for UniScale: shipped data product; for UniWeave: CX playbook adoption).
- Peer or customer feedback.
- Calibration ranking versus other candidates in the same department.

## Typical ladders

Illustrative internal ladders (titles must match an official Position in the HR system):

- Production Technician I → Production Technician II → Production Manager
- IT Support → Data Analyst / Database Administrator → Sr. DBA / Enterprise Architect
- Software Engineer → Senior Software Engineer (when the Position is opened) → engineering manager
- Specialist individual contributor → people manager only if the employee wants a manager track and a vacant ManagerID exists

AIONOS does not create unofficial titles. PositionID in the HR dataset is the system of record.

## Promotion committee

HR chairs a committee with the department head and one peer leader from another unit (for example UniProtect reviews a UniScale promotion) to reduce bias. Diversity job fair origin is never a negative factor.

## Outcomes

- Approved: letter within 7 days, title change on the 1st of the following month, promotional increase per the Salary Raise Policy.
- Deferred: written development plan and a review date no later than the next window.
- Declined: feedback meeting within 10 days; employee may re-apply in the next window.

## High-potential fast track

Employees with PerformanceScore Exceeds, engagement at or above 4.5, EmpSatisfaction at or above 4, and at least 3 special projects may be tagged High Potential. They receive a mentor and may be considered in the September window even if they are only 9 months in role.

## Questions

Employees should ask the assistant what the eligibility checklist is and which ladder applies to their current Position. HR users may ask the assistant to list likely promotion candidates from live employee data.
