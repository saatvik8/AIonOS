# AIONOS Employee Leave Policy

Effective date: 1 April 2026  
Applies to: All full-time and part-time employees of AIONOS and its product units (UniStack, UniWeave, UniScale, UniProtect).

## Purpose

This policy explains how AIONOS employees request, accrue, and use time away from work. Leave is a core part of wellbeing and operational planning. Managers must keep coverage for customer and internal platforms while honouring approved leave.

## Leave year

The leave year runs from 1 April to 31 March. Unused privilege leave may be carried forward as described below. Casual and sick leave reset each leave year and are not encashable.

## Privilege leave (PL / earned leave)

- Full-time employees accrue 1.75 days of privilege leave per completed month, up to 21 days per leave year.
- Part-time employees accrue PL on a pro-rata basis of scheduled hours.
- New hires become eligible to use PL after 90 days of continuous employment, except for pre-approved joining leave documented in the offer letter.
- A maximum of 12 unused PL days may be carried into the next leave year. Any balance above 12 days lapses on 31 March unless an exception is approved by HR for a documented business freeze.
- Encashment of up to 8 unused PL days is allowed at resignation or retirement, calculated on basic salary only.

## Casual leave (CL)

- 8 days of casual leave per leave year.
- Casual leave is for short personal needs (banking, family events, emergencies under one day, etc.).
- No more than 3 consecutive CL days may be taken without manager plus HR approval.
- CL cannot be combined with privilege leave to extend a vacation unless the manager approves in writing.

## Sick leave (SL)

- 12 days of sick leave per leave year.
- Absences of 3 or more consecutive calendar days require a registered medical certificate uploaded in the HR portal.
- Hospitalisation may be covered under additional medical leave of up to 15 days in a year, subject to insurance partner documentation (see Partner Policy).
- Misuse of sick leave (patterned Mondays/Fridays without evidence) may be treated as unauthorised absence and can affect performance ratings.

## Parental leave

- Primary caregiver: 26 weeks of paid parental leave for childbirth or adoption, of which at least 6 weeks should be taken immediately after the event.
- Secondary caregiver: 10 days of paid parental leave to be taken within 6 months of the event.
- Surrogacy and adoptive parents receive the same entitlements as biological parents.
- Employees must notify HR at least 8 weeks before the expected date, or as soon as practicable for adoption.

## Bereavement, marriage, and civic leave

- Bereavement: 5 paid days for immediate family (spouse, child, parent, sibling); 2 paid days for extended family.
- Marriage leave: 5 paid days, once during employment at AIONOS, with at least 15 days’ notice.
- Civic leave: up to 3 paid days per year for jury duty, government ID appointments, or legally required appearances.

## Public holidays

AIONOS observes 12 company-wide public holidays published each December. Employees in customer-facing rotations (UniWeave support, UniProtect SOC) may be rostered on a holiday and receive a compensatory off within 30 days.

## Applying for leave

1. Submit the request in the AIONOS People Portal at least 5 working days in advance for PL, and 1 working day for CL whenever possible.
2. The reporting manager must approve or decline within 2 working days.
3. HR audits unauthorised absence weekly. Unapproved absence is coded as loss of pay and counts toward the attendance metric used in bonus and raise decisions.
4. The HR dataset field **Absences** is the official rolling count of unplanned or unapproved absence days used in performance and bonus calculations.

## Blackout periods

Product release blackouts (typically two weeks around major UniStack and UniProtect releases) may restrict privilege leave to 10% of a team at a time. Blackout calendars are posted by department heads.

## Return to work

After sick leave of 7 or more consecutive days, employees complete a return-to-work check with their manager. Reasonable accommodations are coordinated with HR and UniProtect’s workplace safety partner.

## Questions

Employees should ask the internal assistant about leave balances, notice periods, and documentation. HR Business Partners remain the approvers of policy exceptions.
