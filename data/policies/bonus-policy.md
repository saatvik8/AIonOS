# AIONOS Annual and Spot Bonus Policy

Effective date: 1 April 2026  
Owner: People Operations with Finance.

## Purpose

AIONOS pays variable compensation to reward company performance, department outcomes, and individual contribution across UniStack (AI & Cloud Ops), UniWeave (Customer Experience), UniScale (Data Monetization), and UniProtect (Security & Resilience).

## Eligibility

- Active employees on payroll as of 31 March of the performance year and still employed on the payout date are eligible for the annual bonus.
- Employees who joined after 1 October receive a pro-rata annual bonus based on completed months.
- Employees on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) on 31 March are ineligible for the annual bonus that year, but remain eligible for a later spot bonus if the PIP is closed successfully.
- Terminated-for-cause employees forfeit unpaid bonus. Voluntary exits before payout forfeit the annual bonus unless a written retention exception exists.

## Bonus pools

Company funding is set by the Executive Office after year-end results:

| Company performance vs plan | Pool funding |
| --- | --- |
| Below 85% of plan | 0–40% of target pool |
| 85–99% of plan | 70–95% of target pool |
| 100–110% of plan | 100–110% of target pool |
| Above 110% of plan | up to 125% of target pool |

Department modifiers (+/− 10%) apply for UniStack platform uptime, UniWeave CSAT, UniScale revenue from data products, and UniProtect incident SLA.

## Individual target percentages

Targets are a percentage of annual base salary:

- Executive Office: 25%
- Department heads and senior managers: 18%
- People managers and senior individual contributors (including Sr. DBA, Enterprise Architect, Data Analyst, Software Engineer): 12%
- Professional individual contributors (Production Manager, Accountant, IT Support): 10%
- Production Technicians and equivalent operations roles: 8%

## Performance multiplier

The official **PerformanceScore** in the HR system drives the individual multiplier:

| PerformanceScore | Multiplier |
| --- | --- |
| Exceeds | 1.25 |
| Fully Meets | 1.00 |
| Needs Improvement | 0.50 |
| PIP | 0.00 |

Formula:

**Annual bonus = Base salary × Target % × Company/department funding factor × Performance multiplier × Proration factor**

Attendance adjustment: employees with more than 12 unplanned **Absences** in the performance year receive a 10% reduction to the individual multiplier. More than 18 unplanned absences reduces the multiplier by 25%.

Engagement and special projects: an engagement survey score of 4.5 or higher **and** 3 or more special projects may add a 0.05 discretionary kicker, capped so the final multiplier does not exceed 1.35.

## Payout calendar

- Performance year: 1 January through 31 December.
- Ratings freeze: last performance review on file as of 15 January.
- Bonus letters: last working week of February.
- Payout date: 15 March (or next working day) via payroll.
- Spot bonuses: monthly cycle, paid with the following month’s salary.

## Spot and referral bonuses

- Spot bonus: INR equivalent of USD 250–2,000 for exceptional delivery, customer saves, or security incident response. Manager nominates; HR and Finance approve within 10 days.
- Employee referral: USD 1,000 for a successful full-time hire who completes 90 days; USD 1,500 for critical UniProtect or UniScale roles.
- Diversity job fair hires do not change bonus eligibility; they follow the same performance rules.

## Examples

Example A — Production Technician I, salary 62,506, Fully Meets, company funding 100%, 1 absence:  
62,506 × 8% × 1.00 × 1.00 = 5,000 (rounded to nearest 10 in payroll).

Example B — Software Engineer, salary 95,660, Exceeds, funding 110%, 4 special projects, engagement 5.0:  
95,660 × 12% × 1.10 × 1.30 ≈ 16,400.

## Clawback

Bonus may be clawed back within 12 months for proven policy violation, data leakage, or fabricated performance evidence. UniProtect leads investigations with HR.

## Questions

Employees may ask the assistant for eligibility rules, dates, and how ratings map to payout. Individual bonus letters are issued only by HR. Managers must not quote unofficial numbers.
