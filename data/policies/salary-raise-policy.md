# AIONOS Compensation Review and Salary Raise Policy

Effective date: 1 April 2026  
Owner: Total Rewards (People Operations) and Finance.

## Purpose

AIONOS reviews base pay once a year so compensation stays competitive for AI, cloud, customer experience, data, and security talent, while remaining fair across departments.

## Annual cycle

- Compensation planning window: 1 February to 15 March.
- Merit increases effective: 1 April payroll.
- Off-cycle increases: only for promotion, critical retention, or documented market correction, with HR and department head approval.

## Merit increase guidelines

Managers propose increases inside these bands. HR calibrates across departments so Production, IT/IS, Sales, Software Engineering, Admin Offices, and Executive Office stay internally equitable.

| PerformanceScore | Typical merit increase | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exceeds | 8% to 12% of base | Requires calibration evidence and latest review. |
| Fully Meets | 4% to 7% of base | Default band for solid delivery. |
| Needs Improvement | 0% to 2% of base | Increase is rare and never above 2%. |
| PIP | 0% | No merit increase while PIP is open. |

High unplanned absences (more than 12 in the year) normally cap the merit increase at the bottom of the band.

## Market adjustment

If a role is more than 15% below the AIONOS salary midpoint for that Position (for example Data Analyst, Production Technician II, Database Administrator), Total Rewards may add a market adjustment of 3% to 8% on top of merit, funded from the department pool. Market data is refreshed twice a year with the compensation survey partner listed in the Partner Policy.

## Promotion-linked raises

Promotions follow the Promotion Policy. The promotional increase is 8% to 15% of base or movement to the new band minimum, whichever is higher. Promotional raises can occur off-cycle and are separate from the April merit cycle.

## Living wage and minimums

AIONOS does not pay below the published band minimum for a Position ID. Production Technician I is the reference operations minimum. Any employee below band minimum after the April cycle is auto-corrected in May payroll.

## Salary confidentiality

Employees may discuss their own pay. They must not demand or scrape other employees’ salaries. HR assistants and the internal RAG system expose peer salary data only to HR users. Employee users receive policy rules and their own pay context, never another person’s salary.

## Manager rules

- No informal promises of a raise percentage.
- Recommendations must cite PerformanceScore, engagement survey, special projects, and business impact.
- Equity adjustments for similar roles with a gap above 10% (same Position, same department, comparable tenure) are flagged automatically for HR review.

## Appeals

Employees who believe the April outcome is inconsistent with this policy may appeal in writing within 15 days of the compensation letter. HR responds within 15 working days. Appeals cannot reopen a performance rating; they only check policy application.

## Questions

Ask the assistant about cycle dates, typical bands by rating, and how promotions interact with merit. Exact personal increase letters come only from HR payroll notifications.
