# AIONOS Partners, Vendors, and Employee Benefits Partners Policy

Effective date: 1 April 2026  
Owner: Procurement, People Operations, and UniProtect.

## Purpose

AIONOS works with a defined set of partners for insurance, learning, payroll, cloud, security, and customer experience. Employees need to know which partners are official, what they can access, and how to raise a partner issue. Managers and HR need to know who owns the relationship.

## Official benefits partners

| Partner | What employees get | How to access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Helix Health Insurance | Group medical for employee + 3 dependents, cashless network | Helix portal via People Portal SSO |
| Northstar Life | 3× annual base salary group term life | Automatic enrolment after 30 days |
| PulseCare EAP | 6 confidential counselling sessions per year, 24/7 helpline | pulsecare.aionos.internal |
| LearnGrid | 20 hours of sponsored learning per year; cloud and security certifications reimbursed up to USD 1,200 | LearnGrid catalogue |
| PayOrbit | Payroll, tax forms, bonus letters | PayOrbit employee login |
| RelocateNow | Transfer support when an internal move requires a city change | HR ticket + RelocateNow case |

Coverage waiting period: medical and life cover start on day 31 of employment. EAP is available from day 1.

## Official technology and delivery partners

These partners support product units. Employees must not sign new vendor papers without Procurement.

- Cloud ops (UniStack): Nimbus Cloud (primary), Cedar Container Registry, Beacon Observability.
- Customer experience (UniWeave): Harbor CX Platform, Echo Survey Co (engagement and CSAT instruments).
- Data monetization (UniScale): Lattice Data Exchange (approved external data buyers only), Quilt Catalog.
- Security (UniProtect): Redline SOC Retainers, VaultKey Identity, ShieldMail for incident notification.

Using a non-approved AI, data, or security vendor with company or customer data is a UniProtect policy breach and can lead to termination for cause.

## Partner discounts for employees

Active employees receive:

- 15% on LearnGrid premium nanodegrees after the complimentary 20 hours are used.
- Corporate rate on Helix preventive health checks once per year.
- Preferred mortgage desk via Northstar’s banking affiliate (information only; AIONOS does not guarantee loan approval).

Discounts end on the last working day if employment ends.

## Referring a new partner

Employees may suggest a partner through the Procurement intake form. Evaluation criteria: security review by UniProtect (mandatory), data processing agreement, pricing vs current contract, and a business sponsor at director level. Average review time is 4 weeks. No employee may accept a personal gift above USD 50 from a current or prospective partner.

## Data sharing with partners

HR may share employee name, EmpID, department, and date of hire with benefits partners as needed for enrolment. Salary is shared only with PayOrbit, Northstar (for life cover multiples), and Finance. PerformanceScore, engagement, and absence details are never shared with technology partners.

Employee users of the internal assistant will receive partner names, access paths, and benefit summaries. They will not receive vendor contract values or other employees’ insured salary multiples.

## Incident reporting

- Benefits issue (claim denied, portal down): HR ticket queue “Benefits-Partners” within 2 days.
- Security issue involving a partner system: UniProtect incident channel immediately.
- Performance or ethics concern about a partner: email partners-ethics@aionos.example (internal).

## Annual recertification

Every partner is recertified by 30 September. If a partner fails UniProtect review, employees are migrated within 90 days and the assistant knowledge base is updated.

## Questions

Ask the assistant which partner covers medical claims, certification reimbursement, payroll letters, or whether a tool is an approved UniStack/UniProtect vendor.
